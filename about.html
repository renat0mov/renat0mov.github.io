About Me

I'm a filmmaker and live director based in Aveiro, Portugal, working across documentary, commercial, and branded content, as well as multi-camera live productions for broadcasts, concerts, corporate events, and live streams.

Filmmaking shapes how I approach live productions through visual composition and pacing, while live directing has strengthened my ability to make fast, confident decisions under pressure.
I enjoy projects where creativity and technical execution go hand in hand, always working closely with the people behind them.

If you'd like to work together, I'd love to hear from you.
